Android

Install directly, no app store needed. Sign in with your lehi31.com account.

Download for Android
Checking latest version…
  1. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to allow installs from your browser the first time.
  2. Sign in, then turn on Notify me when something needs me in Settings.
  3. Updates: come back here and install the new file over the old one. Your sign-in is kept.
